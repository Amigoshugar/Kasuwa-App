      How each price is made

      1. CollectPaid agents report twice a day; traders and farmers report by app, WhatsApp or USSD.
      2. CleanEvery report is converted to ₦ per 100 kg. Reports far from the rest are set aside.
      3. CombineA trust-weighted median gives the price; agents count most, anonymous reports least.
      4. ScoreConfidence rises with more reporters and closer agreement. Gaps are estimated and labelled.
      Prices are sample data from the Kasuwa price model. Mudu weights are approximate and vary by market. Hausa labels need review by a native speaker.
